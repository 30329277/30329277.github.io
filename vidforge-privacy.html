Privacy Policy — VidForge

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Information Collection

VidForge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. The app has no backend, no account system, and never contacts any server operated by us or any third party.

2. Local File Processing

VidForge is a video toolkit (split, merge, clip, repair, compress, and format conversion) that runs entirely on your device. Videos you add are processed locally, and the results are saved only where you choose. Your media files are never uploaded anywhere.

3. Network Access

The app contains no telemetry, advertising, or third-party analytics components, and does not access the network. All features work fully offline.

4. Local Data

The app writes logs to a folder inside your Windows local app-data directory (%LOCALAPPDATA%). These logs contain no personal information and can be deleted at any time.

5. Third-Party Components

VidForge bundles FFmpeg, an open-source multimedia framework licensed under LGPL. FFmpeg runs locally on your device and performs no network communication.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated with the new policy and an effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email 30329277@qq.com. Typical response time is 24–48 hours.
