Tube Miter Calculator — Support

Tube Miter Calculator is an offline shop-floor tool for iPhone and iPad. It calculates saw angles, edge lengths and setup drawings for tube and profile cuts, plus everyday woodworking and cutting-plan helpers.

Features

  • Cut Assistant — Square / Miter, Parallel miters, Opposed miters, Pointed / Pointed, Pointed / V-notch and V-notch / V-notch. Shows long, short and center lengths, complementary and supplementary angles, and a dimensioned Live View that can be rotated and flipped.
  • Calculator — basic arithmetic and degree-based trigonometry, with one-tap copy of the result.
  • Compound Cuts — miter and blade-tilt settings for tapered boxes, pyramids and angled frames.
  • Board Feet — lumber volume and cost, with an optional waste factor.
  • Stock Optimizer — a practical cutting plan from raw bar length, kerf and end trim.
  • Projects & reports — save inputs to History and export a PNG report (Save to Photos or Share).
  • Settings — metric (mm) or imperial (in) units, keep screen awake, clear temporary files, reset inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the angles defined?

Miter angles are measured from a square cut: a square cut is 0°. The Live View also shows the complementary (90° − angle) or supplementary values, so you can match the scale on your saw.

How do I switch between millimetres and inches?

Tap the gear icon on the Home screen and choose Metric or Imperial. Current values are converted automatically and the choice is remembered.

Why can't I save a report to Photos?

The app needs "Add Photos Only" permission. Enable it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Tube Miter Calculator.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All calculations and saved projects stay on your device.

Safety Note

Results are setup aids. Always confirm end identity, top/bottom face, blunt-edge side, scrap side and kerf, and check one first-off part before batch cutting.
